## About Me
<span class="bio-name">Zhen Wang (王振)</span> is an associate professor and a Yuanguang Scholar in the School of Artificial Intelligence at Hebei University of Technology, where he supervises doctoral and master's students. He received his Ph.D. from Tianjin University under the supervision of Prof. <a class="bio-name" href="https://cic.tju.edu.cn/faculty/huqinghua/index.html">Qinghua Hu (胡清华)</a>.

## Research Interests

- **Computer Vision:** Visible–infrared image fusion, object detection, person re-identification, person search and tracking.
- **Machine Learning:** Learning with noisy labels and fine-grained domain generalization. Current interests include active perception and collaboration among aerial and ground robots.
